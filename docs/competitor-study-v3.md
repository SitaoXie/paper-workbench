# Reading + notebook competitor study

Reviewed 2026-10-22. This is independent product research for the next local reading release, not a claim of feature parity. Sources below are official product documentation or upstream repositories. alphaXiv was also opened in a separate hidden browser tab; only local navigation and menus were used, with no public comments, account changes, or messages submitted.

## Findings and decisions

| Product / source | Useful behavior | Limitation or tradeoff | Decision for this application |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Zotero reader](https://www.zotero.org/support/pdf_reader) | Selection opens a color choice; a locked tool enables repeated highlighting. Annotations added to notes retain citations and a route back to the source page. | A note and an annotation are different objects; flattening them into a chat transcript loses their spatial meaning. | Persist highlights and optional comments independently of chat. Keep source page, quote, normalized geometry, color, and timestamps. Every note should navigate back to its source. |
| [Zotero annotation storage](https://www.zotero.org/support/kb/annotations_in_database) | Annotation records live outside the PDF, enabling small incremental updates and tags; an explicit annotated-PDF export makes them portable. | Downloading the original PDF alone does not carry database annotations. Changing the underlying PDF can invalidate anchors. | Use local structured records with document fingerprint and normalized anchors. Export readable Markdown and structured JSON; label original-PDF downloads honestly. Do not imply embedded PDF annotations until that export is actually implemented. |
| [Readwise highlights and notes](https://docs.readwise.io/reader/docs/faqs/highlights-tags-notes) | Highlight, tag, and note are distinct actions; keyboard workflows and margin notes keep reading efficient. | The official page says multiple highlight colors are unavailable, and original-view PDFs do not have the same smooth stylus behavior as text view. | Offer a small color palette plus editable notes; do not let colors be the only accessible category signal. Validate original PDF behavior independently of HTML/text behavior. |
| [Readwise Quick Lookup](https://docs.readwise.io/reader/guides/ghostreader/quick-lookup) | Lookup/translation results appear while reading; they stay temporary until the reader chooses to save a highlight with its result. | Saving every transient lookup would produce noisy notes; sending every lookup to chat would break reading flow. | Translation and explanation open next to the passage. Save-to-notes is explicit. Ask opens a separate question session carrying the quote and page. |
| [Readwise custom prompts](https://docs.readwise.io/reader/guides/ghostreader/custom-prompts) | Prompts distinguish document, paragraph, and word scopes. | A general prompt editor can ask too much of a reader who only wants a quick overview. | Provide Quick read / Deep read presets plus an optional focus field. Persist the actual profile, focus, language, source, and model with every generated version. |
| [Readest](https://readest.com/docs), [upstream feature list](https://github.com/readest/readest) | Adjustable typography, progress, highlights, translation, and side-by-side reading are treated as reading features. | Handwriting is listed as planned in the reviewed upstream README, so it is not evidence of delivered support. Rich parallel panes also consume width. | Preserve reading progress, usable line length, and keyboard reachability before adding more panels. Chinese and English should have complete UI labels. Handwriting can be an honest basic ink tool, with undo and persistence, without claiming tablet-quality palm rejection. |
| [Obsidian PDF++](https://github.com/RyotaUshio/obsidian-pdf-plus) | Markdown side notes and links to selected PDF regions keep knowledge portable and source-connected. | Upstream explicitly warns that reliance on private Obsidian APIs may break after updates. | Use documented PDF.js/browser APIs and a versioned export format. Markdown exports must remain useful without this application; include ordinary quotations, page references, and note text. |
| [Sioyek](https://github.com/ahrm/sioyek), [data model documentation](https://github.com/ahrm/sioyek-documentation/blob/main/docs/source/scripting.rst) | Research-oriented navigation keeps locations, marks, highlights, and linked destinations available independently of the prose. | Power-user navigation can become invisible to new users. | Source jumps need visible return behavior and persisted reading position. Keep core annotation controls visible; shortcuts are an enhancement. |
| [alphaXiv example paper](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.04158) | A single AI Overview carries figures and source-page links; language is a top-level control. Manual exploration found a wide notes workspace alongside the paper, and overview provenance/version information in its own menu. | The inspected anonymous notes panel required sign-in to save. The version menu showed provenance and “AI Overview v4”; this does not establish user-selectable arbitrary version history. | One coherent local overview surface; switch saved generations there. Local notes must work without a hosted account. Preserve the broad reading/assistant workspace and meaningful inline figures. |

The limitations above are either directly documented/observed or product-design inferences stated as tradeoffs. They are not an exhaustive quality ranking. This application does not need to reproduce social comments, hosted sync, or subscription billing to offer a strong local reading workflow.

## Proposed information architecture

- **Overview:** one selected reading interpretation. Quick read and Deep read are generation profiles, not competing output cards. The original abstract remains a clearly labeled source section. Existing illustrated summaries and reports migrate into distinguishable versions instead of appearing simultaneously.
- **Paper:** continuous PDF; select → highlight / note / translate / explain / ask. Translation and explanation preserve the reader's current position and show the selected source. They can be copied or saved. Only ask creates a question session.
- **Notebook:** current-paper annotations and notes, filter/search, edit, source jump, export. A document-level note is allowed without a fake text selection. Ink is another annotation kind, not a screenshot substituted for a source anchor.
- **History:** explicit Question sessions / Translations / Explanations / Overview versions. Include paper, page when available, language, and date. Do not mix transient lookups into chat or create empty sessions just to record activity.
- **Language:** complete Chinese / English UI switch independent of the original paper language. Route-based `/zh/` and `/en/` behavior works locally; optional deployment subdomains `zh.example` and `en.example` may choose an initial locale. An explicit user switch takes precedence over inferred locale. Opening a locale route does not trigger generation. Per the requested workflow, an explicit Chinese/English switch translates the selected fixed reading when its target-language version is missing and a model is configured; progress is visible and the original is retained. Subsequent switches reuse the cache.

Quick read should answer the problem, principal result, why it matters, and main caveat in a short scan. Deep read should explain method, evidence, assumptions, limits, and relevant figures with page references. Both can accept a focus prompt such as “compare the experimental controls” or “explain the proof assumptions.” A visual style is secondary to these different reading intentions.

## Independent release gates

1. Highlight two nonadjacent lines, zoom, move far enough to virtualize the page away, return, and reload. The geometry still matches the text; a note opens the same source. Changing paper never shows another paper's annotations.
2. Enter and edit a note, close/reopen the panel, refresh, and export. No silent draft loss or stale response overwrite. Saved state is visible. New document notes do not require a model key.
3. Translate and explain a selection in both language directions. The result is near the source, selectable, and scrollable; it does not unexpectedly open/replace the question session. Save-to-note is deliberate. Errors remain in context and can be retried.
4. Ask about a selection twice. Each explicit new question workflow has the correct paper/quote/page and independently retrievable session; existing conversations remain intact. History category filters actually change their results.
5. Generate quick and deep overviews with a custom focus. Exactly one chosen output is displayed; generation failure preserves the prior version. Switching versions restores their own language/profile/provenance/figures, including legacy records.
6. Change Chinese ↔ English in place and open direct locale routes. Essential controls, empty states, errors, settings, and mobile labels follow the locale; imported original text retains its source language unless translated explicitly.
7. If ink ships: draw, undo, save, zoom, reload, and delete/undo a stroke. Scrolling is possible outside ink mode. Pointer cancellation cannot leave the reader stuck drawing. Browser tests are not a substitute for claiming physical stylus/palm-rejection support.
8. Verify at desktop, narrow desktop, and phone widths: no two competing full-width sidebars, no obscured input/close/export buttons, no text clipped by popups, no loss of the broad assistant from the preceding release.

## Open-source reuse boundary

This study borrows interaction ideas, not implementation code or product assets. [Zotero reader COPYING](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zotero/reader/master/COPYING) identifies AGPLv3; [Readest](https://github.com/readest/readest/blob/main/LICENSE) is AGPLv3; [Sioyek](https://github.com/ahrm/sioyek/blob/main/LICENSE) is GPLv3. Their code must not be copied into a differently licensed package without a deliberate license-compatible decision. [PDF++](https://github.com/RyotaUshio/obsidian-pdf-plus/blob/main/LICENSE) is MIT, with separate upstream third-party notices; any eventual source reuse still needs attribution and dependency review. No such reuse was performed in this study.

For this iteration, maintain the project's own implementation around its existing PDF.js dependency and export stable plain data. Do not bundle private provider endpoints, credentials, local paths, runtime papers, or personal notes in tagged source releases.
