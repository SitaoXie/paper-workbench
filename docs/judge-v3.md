# Independent reading-workspace judge

Review date: 2026-10-22. Final verdict: **pass within the reviewed release scope**. All blocking findings raised in this review have been corrected and rechecked, including a real English interactive translation after two failed earlier approaches. This review uses direct browser interaction, not test counts as a substitute for product judgment. It is not a claim that the entire product is bug-free or universally superior to alphaXiv or Zotero.

## Reference inspection

The judge independently opened the [alphaXiv example paper](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.04158), opened its assistant, switched to the PDF, scrolled to the following page, and opened its notes workspace. The useful baseline is a coherent overview with relevant figures and page citations, continuous PDF scrolling, a broad assistant pane, and an easy switch to notes. Anonymous saving in the inspected notes pane required sign-in. No public comments or messages were posted.

The judge also inspected the official [Zotero PDF reader documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/pdf_reader). Its source-linked annotations, explicit note editing, and separation between annotation storage and PDF export are relevant goals. A local sidecar notebook is useful, but does not by itself establish Zotero interoperability or embedded-PDF export parity. Further upstream research is recorded in [the competitor study](competitor-study-v3.md).

A later alphaXiv pass explicitly toggled Light and Dark on the same paper and inspected the figure on page 2. The PDF itself follows the theme: dark paper, light text, and transformed chart backgrounds, with approximately preserved chart hues. The original Dark setting was restored. The inspected toolbar did not expose an independent original-color switch. A local original-color override is therefore a useful improvement for chart verification. The original also offers a target-language picker and model picker directly in the selection popover, plus compact page-position marks beside the continuous PDF.

## Scope and non-blocking limits

This is a browser review on one available text-based research PDF and responsive viewport simulation. It does not establish physical stylus behavior, palm rejection, mobile operating-system keyboard behavior, scanned-PDF OCR, or universal model accuracy. The ink tool should be described as basic pointer drawing until those device tests exist.

The second browser pass used an independent Chrome tab because the in-app browser surface was unavailable. Third-party browser-extension selection widgets were visible in that tab and are not counted as application defects.

The initial original-abstract Explain attempt returned a model-connectivity error; the later real UI request succeeded and was checked in history. The new scientific candidate and replacement visual interpretation passed content review, and theme-following PDF behavior passed actual UI checks. The candidate was published and its interactive answer/source actions passed; the viewport clamp passed. A later real English article translation succeeded after roughly two minutes. Its pending state survived a source-page round trip, and switching Chinese/English reused the article immediately on the same version. The final real interactive retry also succeeded after the field-only translation repair. Its checked answers, source binding, and bilingual cache round trip passed. No blocking issue remains in these reviewed workflows. Offline visual execution is limited by the browser policy described above. No provider credentials were inspected or changed. Existing test results and other agents' checks supplement these observations, but must not be represented as this judge's independent manual verification.

The final-pass viewport override did not visibly resize the Chrome tab, so this pass does not claim a fresh manual phone-width check of the new interactive version. Earlier mobile reader/assistant checks remain valid for their reviewed builds. The implementation agent reports a final 80-test browser suite, including desktop, mobile, and offline checks, plus 72 Node tests; those are supplementary evidence, not this judge’s direct browser verification.

The revised composer has no separate subscription/disclaimer rows underneath. Its bottom is close to the persistent assistant bubble’s bottom. The Code2 subscription-task icon was clicked and returned the Task copied state.

The translation failure history is retained for accuracy: the intermediate build correctly retained the English article while labeling interactive translation incomplete, disabling Interactive and offline HTML export. The judge's explicit retry kept its pending state across PDF navigation and a Chinese/English round trip, then failed with “Interactive source bindings or structure changed and could not be repaired.” The error survived another PDF round trip. The final implementation instead translated named display fields and merged them into the original structure; the next actual UI retry succeeded, cleared the error, and enabled both controls. The root agent separately confirmed all immutable bindings matched and the existing English article hash and creation time were unchanged. Chinese article emphasis in the opening conclusion and the “487 (12.7%)” passage now renders as actual strong text without visible Markdown delimiters.

A non-blocking localization follow-up remains: the English article retains a Chinese generated figure caption even though the surrounding article is English. Source-image labels should remain original, but generated display captions could have translated variants or an explicit source-language label.

Model-output reliability remains an ongoing limit. Existing question history still contains an earlier answer that overstates unchanged strategy support; later reviewed answers and the published v7 explain the evidence boundary correctly. Preserving history must not imply certifying every prior model response. The application should keep source checking and qualified interpretation central to the reading workflow.

Final local evidence: `release/judge-v3-interactive-english.png` shows the real English interactive result, and `release/judge-v3-dark-reader-assistant-zh.png` shows the final compact Chinese reader and existing question session. Earlier failure screenshots are retained as review history, not the final product state.
